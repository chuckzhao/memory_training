### 📚 Story Details

**Read this story once, then answer questions**:

"The Mystery of the Missing Cookies"

Tommy was 10 years old and loved chocolate chip cookies. One Tuesday afternoon at 3:30 PM, he came home from Washington Elementary School to find that someone had eaten 5 cookies from the cookie jar on the kitchen counter. The jar was blue with white stripes. Tommy had three suspects: his 7-year-old sister Lily who was wearing a green shirt, his 15-year-old brother Jake who was playing video games, and Max, their golden retriever dog who had chocolate on his nose. Tommy found crumbs leading to the backyard.

**Questions**:
1. How old is Tommy?
2. What day was it?
3. What time did he get home?
4. What school does he go to?
5. How many cookies were eaten?
6. What color is the cookie jar?
7. What's his sister's name and age?
8. What was Jake doing?
9. What kind of dog is Max?
10. Where did the crumbs lead?

## Ages 11-13 Practice

### 📖 Complex Story Memory

**Read once, then answer questions**:

"The Science Fair"

On Friday, March 15th, at Riverside Middle School, the annual science fair took place in the gymnasium from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Twelve-year-old Maya Rodriguez entered a project about renewable energy, specifically focusing on solar power and wind turbines. She built a model that demonstrated how a house could use 60% solar panels and 40% wind energy to power all its electricity needs.

Her best friend, thirteen-year-old Alex Chen, created a project on marine biology, studying how plastic pollution affects sea turtles in the Pacific Ocean. He displayed 15 photographs he took during his family's vacation to California, showing 3 different species of sea turtles.

The judges were: Dr. Jennifer Williams (a chemistry professor), Mr. Robert Thompson (a physics teacher), and Ms. Sarah Martinez (an environmental scientist). First place won a $500 prize and a trophy. Maya's project placed second, earning $300, while Alex's project placed third, earning $150. The first-place winner was eighth-grader Daniel Park with his project on artificial intelligence and machine learning.

The fair had 45 total projects divided into 5 categories: Physics (8 projects), Chemistry (10 projects), Biology (12 projects), Environmental Science (9 projects), and Technology (6 projects).

**Questions** (20 total):
1. What day and date was the fair?
2. What school?
3. Where in the school?
4. What time did it start and end?
5. How old is Maya?
6. What's Maya's last name?
7. What was her project about?
8. What percentage was solar vs wind?
9. How old is Alex?
10. What's Alex's last name?
11. What was his project about?
12. How many photos did he display?
13. How many turtle species?
14. Name all three judges.
15. What's first place prize?
16. What place did Maya get?
17. What place did Alex get?
18. Who won first place?
19. What was the winning topic?
20. How many total projects, and how were they divided by category?

### 🗺️ Multi-Step Directions

**Treasure Hunt Directions**:

Starting from the old lighthouse:
1. Walk 50 steps north toward the pine tree grove
2. Turn east at the large boulder with a red mark
3. Continue for 30 steps until you reach the creek
4. Cross the wooden bridge (12 planks long)
5. Turn south and walk past 3 oak trees
6. Look for a stone wall (approximately 6 feet tall)
7. Count 15 stones from the left side
8. Dig beneath the 15th stone
9. The treasure chest is 2 feet deep
10. Inside you'll find: a gold compass, 3 silver coins, an old map, and a ruby ring

**Remember**: All 10 steps in order!
