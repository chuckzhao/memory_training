## Tips for Success

**Chunking Strategy:**
- 5 digits: Split as 2-3 or 3-2 (739-24)
- 7 digits: Split as 3-4 or 2-2-3 (583-9271)
- 10 digits: Split as 3-3-4 or use phone number format
- 12 digits: Split as 3-3-3-3 or 4-4-4

**Visualization:**
- Imagine the numbers on a keypad
- Associate with dates or ages
- Create a story with the numbers

**Practice Schedule:**
- Start with Level 1
- Master 4/5 sequences before moving up
- Practice daily for best results
