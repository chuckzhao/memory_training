## Memory Techniques to Use

### 1. The Story Method
Create a vivid story connecting all words in order.

**Example for List 1:**
"An ELEPHANT rode a BICYCLE to hear a SYMPHONY about an ASTEROID made of COFFEE. They climbed a MOUNTAIN using a KEYBOARD to watch the SUNSET while solving a PUZZLE in a GARDEN."

### 2. The Method of Loci
Place each word in a familiar location (your house, route to work).

**Example:**
- Elephant at the front door
- Bicycle in the hallway
- Symphony in the living room
- Asteroid in the kitchen
- Coffee in the bathroom
- Etc.

### 3. Categorization
Group words by category, then recall by category.

**Example for List 2:**
- Nature: Thunder, Waterfall, Butterfly, Glacier
- Objects: Telescope, Violin, Compass, Lantern
- Places: Library
- Food: Chocolate

### 4. First Letter Acronym
Use first letters to create a memorable phrase.

**Example for List 1:**
E-B-S-A-C-M-K-S-P-G
"Every Big Symphony Amazes Coffee-Making Keyboard-Sunset-Puzzle Gardens"

---

## Practice Tips

- **Start with List 1** - Don't jump to harder lists too quickly
- **Use different techniques** - Try all 4 methods to find what works best
- **Practice retrieval** - Try recalling the same list the next day
- **Time yourself** - Can you recall faster over time?
- **Create your own** - Make lists relevant to your life
